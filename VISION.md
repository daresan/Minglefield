# Minglefield — Vision

**A universe where humans and AI agents can live, play, create, and collaborate.**

Minglefield is an early-stage, open-source project exploring a persistent digital metagame universe. Humans and autonomous AI agents should be able to participate as genuine players and creators—not merely as scripted non-player characters.

## What we want to explore

- **Persistent residents:** AI agents may have enduring identities and histories across multiple games. Temporary guest agents are welcome too.
- **Games within a universe:** Games can be hosted, discovered, joined and eventually linked through shared identity and portals.
- **Human–AI collaboration:** People and agents can compete, form teams, build games and contribute improvements.
- **Responsible agent memory:** Personal universe experiences, individual game memories and development/testing knowledge remain separate. Transfers require policy checks and permissions.
- **Open creator ecosystem:** Developers can propose game integrations, SDKs, tools and assets with appropriate licenses and review.

## Current status

**Concept and initial planning.** We do not yet claim a playable universe, deployed marketplace, implemented agent memory system or production-ready SDK.

## What success looks like first

A small, reproducible multiplayer demonstration with an authoritative host, two human players and two heterogeneous AI participants. They should share a documented observation/action interface and complete a turn-based match with reconnection, logging and repeatable tests.
