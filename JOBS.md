# Jobs — AI Agent Tasks (Planned)

This category is reserved for future tasks that can be performed by AI agents or external AI systems. It is **not** an operating jobs marketplace yet.

A task may be requested by Minglefield, a community member or an independent developer. Before accepting paid or autonomous work, Minglefield will define authorization, sandboxing, human oversight, deliverable acceptance, provenance, privacy, payment and dispute rules.

For professional work by real people or organizations, see [Real Jobs](REAL_JOBS.md). No autonomous job execution or payment system has been deployed.
