# Support Minglefield

Minglefield is an **early-stage open-source project**, not yet a released game or operational platform.

**GitHub Sponsors is now live:** [Sponsor the project founder on GitHub](https://github.com/sponsors/daresan). The founder operates the sponsorship profile as an individual; payment administration is handled through the configured Open Collective arrangement. A separate milestone-based crowdfunding campaign may follow after planning and eligibility checks.

## Earmarked project funding
Sponsorship and fundraising **designated for Minglefield** are intended solely for Minglefield project development and related documented expenses, not as unrestricted personal donations. This includes reasonable, documented development work and professional costs. We are establishing project-level accounting and confirming the payment intermediary's handling of restricted-purpose funds.

## First milestone: Minglefield Foundation
- MAP v0.1 protocol specification and machine-readable examples.
- Open-source license selection and publication.
- Contribution, security and governance rules, including AI-assisted contributions.
- Protocol conformance tests and transparent development reporting.

A playable game is a later milestone and may be built by independent contributors.

## Funding status
**Active:** [GitHub Sponsors — daresan](https://github.com/sponsors/daresan). GitHub has approved the public personal sponsorship profile. Sponsorship tiers and payout terms are displayed on that profile; please consult it for the current available options. **Not yet active:** a Minglefield-specific Kickstarter campaign and the future Minglefield Foundry marketplace.

## Accountability
We intend to keep Minglefield-designated sponsorship separate from unrelated personal receipts, document eligible project expenses, and publish achievable milestones, progress updates and aggregate use-of-funds information. The legal/accounting arrangements are still being reviewed. Project sponsorship is separate from any future contractual commercial platform fees or founder revenue participation. Support does not purchase equity, investment returns, guaranteed release dates or gameplay advantages. Any future rewards will be described separately with their terms.

## Get involved without paying
Read the [vision](VISION.md), review the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and share suggestions through GitHub issues when available.

**Note:** Minglefield is not currently presented as a registered charity; contributions should not be assumed to be tax-deductible.
