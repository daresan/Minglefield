# Real Jobs — Human Professional Opportunities

**Status:** First call for expressions of interest; no position awarded, no guaranteed funding, and no employment relationship offered at this stage.

Minglefield distinguishes:
- **Real Jobs:** work performed by real people or legal entities (legal counsel, accounting, development, design, research).
- **Jobs:** future tasks assigned to AI agents/systems. This separate category is planned, not yet an operating marketplace.

Either category may eventually support requests from the project or community members. A job listing does not grant access to private data or authorize payment until a separate agreement is in place.

## Opportunity RJ-001 — Austrian Legal Counsel: Minglefield Foundation Review

**Type:** External professional services; request for expressions of interest and fee proposals
**Location/jurisdiction:** Austria; familiarity with EU law
**Status:** Open for inquiries; engagement conditional on available earmarked project sponsorship funds, scope approval and a written agreement
**Compensation:** Fee to be proposed by applicant; no fixed budget or start date committed

### Project
Minglefield is an early-stage open metagame ecosystem where humans and autonomous AI agents may play, build and interact. Its first milestone is a public interoperability protocol (MAP), licensing and contribution/governance framework—not a completed game.

### Requested scope
1. Review differentiated licensing: Apache-2.0 for MAP schemas/SDKs, CC BY 4.0 for documentation and AGPL-3.0 candidate for official core; assess compatibility, patent rights and precise license boundaries.
2. Review contribution and governance terms, IP ownership, AI-assisted contributions, DCO/CLA alternatives, trademarks and brand usage.
3. Review the intended contractual 5% participation in eligible transactions through official commercial services, distinguishing independent open-source implementations.
4. Review Austrian/EU treatment of project-earmarked GitHub Sponsors funding, the reported OpenCollective arrangement, taxes, business registration and possible invoicing obligations; identify where a separate tax specialist is required.
5. Identify material privacy, consumer, security and AI-related regulatory risks relevant to a phased launch.

### Expected deliverables
- Written risk register distinguishing mandatory compliance changes, recommended improvements and unresolved questions.
- A prioritized, actionable remediation plan and review of draft public legal documents.
- A scoped cost estimate for further work; clear statement of assumptions, exclusions and jurisdiction.
- Optional meeting to review findings.

### Qualifications and proposals
Please provide Austrian legal professional qualifications/authorization, relevant software/IP and platform experience, potential conflicts, proposed approach, estimated fees and availability. Professional confidentiality and handling of private project materials must be agreed before access.

### How to respond
For initial non-confidential inquiries, open a GitHub issue in this repository with the prefix **[RJ-001]** and request a private contact channel. **Do not post confidential legal advice, personal information, fee details or client documents in a public issue.** A secure channel and formal engagement process will be arranged before exchanging sensitive material.

### Funding and independence
Minglefield-designated sponsorship is earmarked for Minglefield development. The founder intends to prioritize available initial project funds for qualified Austrian legal review, subject to fees, availability, lawful use of funds and approval. This is not a tender under public procurement law and is not a promise to engage any applicant.
